Professional Highlights

The Horseshoe — Rapidan Estate Litigation Successfully litigated and closed “the Horseshoe,” the former Rapidan estate of Secretary of State Stettinius, on behalf of the largest landowner in Orange County, by enforcing his contract with the Governor of West Virginia, James Justice.
World Trade Center & $25M Manassas Lease Negotiations Represented the global leader in interbank secure financial messaging (based in Belgium) in successful lease negotiation for two floors of the former World Trade Center; also successfully negotiated a $25 million leased site in Manassas, VA on the client’s behalf.
$70M+ Agricultural Finance — Madison County Represented Madison County’s largest cattle and farming operation of over 4,000 acres in complex commercial loan transactions exceeding $70 million, together with its business and liability issues.
International Direct Mail Counsel Represented an international leader in direct mail operations in all business matters.
Multi-State Fitness Facility Expansion Assisted the business expansion of a holding company to launch over a dozen out-of-state athletic and fitness facilities.
Complex Commercial Litigation & Condemnation Served as lead counsel in complex commercial litigation including construction litigation, mechanics lien litigation, and other business disputes; represented the Commonwealth of Virginia on local condemnation matters.
Land Use & Real Estate Transactions Extensive experience in land use, zoning, and commercial and residential real estate transactions; consistently conducting successful contract negotiations and dispute resolution.
